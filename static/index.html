How it works. Every hour, Polymarket runs a "Bitcoin Up or Down" market that resolves Up if the BTC/USDT 1-hour candle closes ≥ its open (source: Binance). The model estimates that probability as Φ( ln(price/open) / (σ·√τ) ) — a driftless-random-walk barrier probability, with σ the realised hourly volatility and τ the fraction of the hour left. When the model's number differs from the market's price by more than the edge threshold, it takes a capped fractional-Kelly paper position and settles it on the real candle.
Honesty notes. The equity curve is seeded with a fair-odds backtest over the last 36 real hours (expected value ≈ 0 — it shows variance, not profit), then extends with live paper trades. A real edge here is thin and often negative after crossing the spread. Nothing here places a real trade or constitutes financial advice. Data: Binance & Polymarket public APIs.
