Read the confidence score honestly. It is a heuristic for how notable and tradeable something looks right now — not the probability you will make money. Markets: a side is suggested only for crypto up/down markets (on paper, real model edge); elsewhere just the market's own odds. Assets (stocks, forex, crypto, commodities): there is no independent model, so the Bullish/Bearish lean is a transparent momentum-plus-news heuristic — a computed indicator, not advice. News is context, no order is ever placed, and none of this is financial advice.

How the score is built. Each market's confidence is 100 × Σ(weight × component) over the coloured components above (crypto up/down markets weight the model edge; other markets reweight across the remaining factors). The bar under each score shows that exact mix. News comes from public RSS at reputable outlets (BBC, MarketWatch, Yahoo Finance, NPR), keyword-matched to each market and treated strictly as untrusted context — headlines are shown so you can judge them, and sentiment is a crude word-list heuristic, not an NLP model. Nothing here is financial advice, and no real order is ever placed.
