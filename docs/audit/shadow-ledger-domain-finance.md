## What holds
- None of the new strings uses will, should, buy, sell, profit, guarantee, certain or edge. No new figure appears without the existing paper/before-costs note (SH_NOTE, `ui/app.py:594-600`). Sample size sits on the look line ({N}, {used}) and in the corner line ({k}).
- The robust-crossing rule (spec:83-84, ADR:232-234) only removes crossings, so it cannot add a claimed link. That fits the brief.
- `no_risk` resolves the earlier P3-5 (a zero stop distance shown as "no closing price").

## Findings
**P2-1. The corner sentence misdescribes the computation and implies a proven range.** spec:99-100. "If the {k} left out had all gone the other way: z from {lo} to {hi}." The corners assign target or stop by score; they do not reverse actual outcomes. `left` members have a known hand-close result, and `no_price` members have no result at all. "From … to" reads as a guaranteed interval, but spec:84-86 and 185-186 say it is not a proven bound on z. A retail user reads a certain range, and a high corner past ±2.315 next to "no link strong enough" reads as "the score might work". Fix, plain: "The {k} left out have no usable result. Filled in at the extremes (higher scores at the target and lower at the stop, then the reverse), z would be {lo} and {hi}. These are extreme cases, not estimates." Expert: "{k} excl.: corner z {lo} / {hi} (extremes, not a bound)".

**P2-2. "Every R lies in [−1, +R:R]" is false when the fill differs from the planned entry.** ADR:232, spec:79-81. `pos.entry = price` sets entry to the fill price (`sonar/portfolio.py:419`). R is then `d*(exit−entry)/|entry−stop|` (`sonar/calibration.py:448-453`). A long planned at 100/95/110 that fills at 97 has R at the target of 6.5, not 2. A fill through the stop gives a positive risk with the stop on the wrong side, so R at the stop can be +1. This is the same root as the earlier P3-7. Scenario: the corners are narrower than the true extremes, so a crossing can count as robust when it is not, and then the crossed-up line ("higher scores went with higher R") is shown. Fix: give each excluded member its own bounds, [R at the stop, R at the target], computed from its entry, stop and target, which are all known at entry. Change ADR:232 to "Each member's R lies between its R at the stop and its R at the target, both known at entry; the corners use these." The model-validator should confirm this.

**P2-3. A look past the line but failing the corners has no consistent status line.** spec:100-102. If `SH_LOOK_NOT_CROSSED` (`ui/app.py:520-522`) is reused, it says "no link strong enough to cross the line yet. z = 2.6; the line is ±2.315", which contradicts itself. If the crossed line is reused, "crossed the line" comes first. "Not in every case" is vague, and "it does not count" can read as the app suppressing a good result. Fix: a separate key. Plain: "Look {k} of 3, first {N} entries ({used} with a result): z = {z} is past the line, but with the {k} left out filled in at the extremes it is not past it in both cases. This look does not count as crossed; the test goes on." Add `SH_CAVEAT_CROSSED`, because a z past the line is visible. Expert: "Look {k}/3: |z| ≥ 2.315 but corners not; not crossed ({k} excl.)".

**P2-4. An unreadable ledger silently brings back the problem the spec fixes.** spec:108-110 and 154-156, ADR:227-228. On a read error the pass "runs as v2.260 does". A look recorded earlier is then recomputed from that day's closes and shown, possibly with a different z or crossing (validator P1-1). The only warning is "Results are worked out but not recorded." Fix, plain: "The record of results could not be read ({reason}), so it is left untouched for a person to check. What is shown is worked out again today and may differ from what was recorded before; nothing new is recorded." In the read-error state, also hold back any crossing and the "The test ends here" wording.

**P2-5. A "constants" mismatch still says "The recorded result stands".** spec:105-107 and ADR:246-248. "constants" means T, the looks, the boundary or the grace changed: the test is no longer the one fixed in advance. Saying the record "stands" under a changed design hides that. Fix, for {why} = constants: "The test's fixed settings have changed since look {k} was recorded on {date}. The recorded result belongs to the old settings, and the new ones need their own record." For plain mode, translate {why}: "members" becomes "which entries it counts", "z" becomes "the z figure", "constants" becomes "the test's fixed settings".

**P2-6. Moving the ledger aside restarts the record with no trace.** spec:175-178, ADR:243-245. "Move the file aside" is recommended and stays manual. Done after a disappointing look without a book reset, it allows a silent re-run of a look. Pre-registration loses its meaning, and a reader is misled about how many tries a crossing took. The file is write-once by code only: it is not tamper-evident. Fix: show `created_at` in the readout ("Record kept since {date}."), and archive under a dated name rather than delete. On the read side, flag any archived ledger found ("an earlier record from {date} exists"). Also state in the ADR that the ledger is not tamper-evident and that the daily and off-site backups are the only cross-check.

**P3-1. The pre-registration claim is narrowly true and easy to over-read.** ADR:218-220. "no time outcome has been computed or seen" is true of time exits. But the book outcomes of entries settled before T, and the hand closes (`left`), are already visible in the paper book. The corner rule changes how `left` is treated. Fix: "No shadow look and no time-exit outcome has been computed. Book outcomes of entries closed before T, including hand closes, are visible in the paper book and were not consulted for this amendment." The last clause can only be confirmed by the owner (not verified).

**P3-2. "The recorded result stands" reads as "verified correct".** spec:105. Fix: "Worked out again today, look {k} comes out differently ({why}). As fixed in advance, the result recorded on {date} is kept. Either one may rest on a data error." Expert: "look {k}: recomputation differs ({why}); recorded {date} kept by design".

**P3-3. "Waits for them until {date} at the latest" omits what happens next.** spec:103-104. The look also needs a running holder, so "at the latest" overstates. The {date} must be the maximum t_T + 28 days over the waiting members. Fix: "… waits for them until {date}; any still without a price then are left out and the look goes ahead." Expert: "… until {date}, then excluded".

**P3-4. "no stop" (expert) reads as "no stop-loss order, unlimited risk".** spec:111. Fix, plain: "{r} whose stop was at the entry price (no R can be worked out)". Expert: "{r} risk = 0".

**P3-5. "`no_risk` … unrelated to the outcome" is not certain under fill pricing.** spec:90-92, ADR:239-241. `risk = abs(entry−stop)` uses the fill price (`portfolio.py:419`, `calibration.py:448`). `risk = 0` therefore means a fill exactly at the stop, which is next to the outcome. This is rare and the book has none (spec:92, not verified). Fix: say "known at fill", and include `no_risk` in the corners like any other exclusion (spec:87 already counts it as left out). Ask the model-validator to confirm.

**P3-6. Ordering on the look line.** spec:97-98. Put "Recorded {date}" before z, so the date the figure is fixed reads first: "Look 1 of 3, recorded {date}: …".

## Verdict
No P0 or P1. Six P2s should be resolved in the spec and ADR before the owner's gate, or explicitly deferred by the owner. P2-2 and P3-5 also go to the model-validator. The jurisdiction question and any performance-advertising judgment need a qualified human.
