## Changes
- **Status line at any width (U-1):** every status write goes through `_set_status`, mirrored to a
  window status bar shown while the rail is folded below 1420 px (it used to vanish with the rail's
  foot: trade results, errors, read-only notices).
- **Undo (U-6):** after Buy/Short/Close a toast offers Undo for 8 s. `Portfolio.cancel` removes a
  just-opened position and refunds a long's cash; `Portfolio.reopen` reverses a just-made manual
  close. Neither reaches the graded record. Refused when: past 30 s; the price has moved since
  (else a bad start could vanish, or a reopened close be graded TARGET with hindsight); a long's
  reopen lacks the cash; a protocol entry; a pending or asynchronously filled position; a barrier
  exit ("it already closed at its stop/target"). `Live.undo` forwards to the holder when following;
  `POST /api/undo` (Host/Origin rules apply).
- **Start here (U-3):** a card on the landing page while the book is empty, until the first trade or
  "Got it" (kept in `<data>/onboarding.json`, and in memory if that cannot be written).
- **Keyboard (U-4, part):** ⌘1–⌘8 reach each page in rail order.
